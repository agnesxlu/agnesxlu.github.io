About Me

Hello, 你好 👋

I'm Xueer, a bilingual journalist at Mission Local, a nonprofit newsroom in San Francisco. I build the newsroom's webpages, dashboards and interactive tools as it grows, and I write about the Excelsior neighborhood and a variety of topics like culture, small businesses, government and politics.

I love building tools that make content more efficient and engaging, and exploring how AI can help along the way.

I started in journalism at Nanjing University's student publications in 2018 in China, worked as a tech reporter and social media lead in Beijing, and moved to California in 2021.
