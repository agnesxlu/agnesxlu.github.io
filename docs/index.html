Thanks for stopping by! I'm Xueer Lu. I'm a bilingual journalist and communications professional based in the San Francisco Bay Area, with a background in data journalism, multimedia storytelling, and multi-channel communications.

At Mission Local, a nonprofit newsroom in San Francisco, I cover politics, government, housing, and community affairs. Alongside my reporting, I support the newsroom's communications work, produce bilingual content, and build webpages and dashboards as the organization continues to grow. My work sits at the intersection of clear writing, data, and community engagement.

I hold a Master of Journalism from UC Berkeley's Graduate School of Journalism and a Graduate Certificate in Applied Data Science. My thesis investigated a piece of legislation aiming to reduce microplastic pollution in Monterey Bay, combining data analysis, environmental reporting, and policy tracking. I've been living in California since 2021.

I'm drawn to organizations and companies where storytelling serves a larger mission, whether that's informing a community, advancing a cause, or making complex ideas accessible to the people who need them most.

You can check out my resume here or check out my recent writing at Mission Local here. You can also reach out to me via email at agnesxueerlu at gmail dot com.

I'm now open to communications, nonprofit development, editorial, and content roles.
